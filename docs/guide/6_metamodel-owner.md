# Metamodel owner

You decide what the repository can hold: the element types, the relationships
allowed between them, their attributes, and where the enterprise level ends.

## What you look after

- **Types and relationships.** A type earns its place the way an element does:
  by the relationships it has to other types.
- **The level.** Each type sits at the enterprise level, or below it — a
  system's inside, linked rather than modelled. The assistant asks about every
  new element of a type below the level. Set it on the Metamodel page.
- **Versions.** A change to the metamodel is a new version, tried in an
  organisation of its own before it is applied. A published version is frozen
  in what it defines.
- **Templates.** The proposal templates your organisation keeps are typed in
  your metamodel: a table under a type's name holds that type.

## How you work

1. **Take a draft** of the published version.
2. **Change it,** and check that the content held today still fits it.
3. **Try it** in an organisation of its own.
4. **Publish it,** then apply it to the organisation.
